Advanced Water Treatment For Energy Systems

Enabling Oilfield Produced Water Reuse.

Oleava Technologies is a University of Toronto spin-off delivering next-generation oil–water separation for produced water in upstream and midstream operations. Built and validated with U.S. and Canadian agencies, our modular, low-energy systems capture the micro- and nano-emulsions that limit conventional treatment performance. Designed for reliability in onshore and offshore environments, Oleava enables operators to meet evolving regulatory requirements while turning produced water into a reusable, cost-effective resource. Oleava is the pivot point that transforms produced water from a disposal endpoint into a reuse-ready input for the next generation of energy and water systems.

But Oleava did not begin as a product—it began as a problem. Across energy basins worldwide, operators face tightening disposal constraints, rising water demand, and treatment systems pushed beyond their original design limits. We saw a persistent gap between what conventional infrastructure could deliver and what modern operations require: predictable, reliable performance under real field variability. That realization shaped our mission. We set out to build a system that operators could trust—not just in the lab, but in the field, every day. Today, Oleava sits at the intersection of compliance, reliability, and economics—helping transform water management from a regulatory burden into an operational advantage.

Come explore how we got here and how we can help solve your next water challenge.
